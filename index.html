About the Conclave

Celebrating Indian Business Leadership

Utkrishta is the Annual Management Conclave organized by the Institute of Management, Nirma University (IMNU). The conclave serves as a vital bridge between academic excellence and real-world corporate leadership by introducing students to the forefront of industry transformation.

Organized in collaboration with IMNU’s five management clubs: Niche (Marketing), Imprintz (Human Resources), Optimus (Operations), Finesse (Finance), and Clique (IT & Analytics). The conclave provides direct access to C-suite perspectives and strategic industry insights.

“As India navigates a dynamic global landscape shaped by technological disruption, geopolitical shifts, and evolving consumer aspirations, corporate leadership must act as a catalyst for national progress towards Viksit Bharat 2047.”
Utkrishta ‘26 · Theme Statement
The 2026 Theme

Towards Viksit Bharat 2047:
India Inc’s Pivotal Role

As India approaches the centenary of independence in 2047, the vision of Viksit Bharat seeks to establish the nation as globally competitive, innovation-driven, socially inclusive, and sustainable. Utkrishta ‘26 explores how Indian enterprises are architecting this future.

01
Innovative & Resilient
Driving enterprise-wide innovation and building resilient organizations capable of sustaining long-term growth.
02
Responsible & Sustainable
Embedding ESG principles and purpose-driven corporate governance for sustainable societal value creation.
03
Technology-Enabled
Harnessing AI, advanced analytics, and digital ecosystems to transform core business functions.
04
Globally Competitive
Positioning India Inc on the world stage through operational excellence and strategic market agility.

  • Data-driven operational decision-making

Leveraging digital innovation, AI-powered insights, and analytics to build enduring brands, enhance consumer trust, and position India as a global marketing powerhouse.
